# Code of Conduct

**Version 2.5** · First published 2 February 2019 · Last updated 17 October 2026

Pigeon Studios' projects are used and built by server owners, developers, players, Discord communities and customers. We want every space around them to be welcoming, useful and free of drama for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

This Code of Conduct applies to every Pigeon Studios repository and project on GitHub and GitLab, public or private, whatever kind of project it is: issues, pull and merge requests, discussions, reviews, comments, commit messages, wikis, snippets and security advisories. Our [Acceptable Use & Community Rules](https://pigeonstudios.co.uk/legal/acceptable-use/) apply to our website, our Discord server and our hosted services, and say the same things in more detail.

## Our standards

**Do:**

- be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to coding, FiveM, Discord bots or Git;
- assume good intent, and ask before assuming someone is wrong;
- give feedback on the code, not the person, and explain the why;
- accept feedback gracefully, including a "no" to a feature;
- keep discussions on topic and in English, so maintainers and other users can follow them; and
- credit other people's work and ideas.

**Don't:**

- harass, bully, threaten, insult or demean anyone;
- post hate speech or discriminate against anyone because of their race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sex, gender identity or expression, sexual orientation, disability, age or any other characteristic;
- post sexual, violent or shocking content. Sexual content involving minors is never allowed and will be reported to the authorities;
- share anyone's personal information, such as their real name, address, IP address or photos, without their permission (doxxing);
- spam, advertise your own server, store, bot or resources, or post off-topic links;
- share, request or link to leaked, cracked or pirated resources, ours or anyone else's;
- post exploits for other people's servers, or publish security problems in our resources publicly instead of following our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md);
- impersonate Pigeon Studios, our staff or anyone else;
- argue with a moderation decision in public threads (see [Appeals](#appeals)); or
- encourage anyone else to do any of these things.

## Maintainers' responsibilities

Pigeon Studios maintainers are responsible for applying this Code of Conduct fairly. They may edit, hide, lock or delete comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break it, and may block anyone who breaks it from our repositories, temporarily or permanently.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this Code of Conduct, or makes you feel unsafe or unwelcome:

- email pigeon@pigeonstudios.co.uk with the subject line **"Conduct report"**, including links or screenshots; or
- message our staff in our [Discord server](https://discord.gg/2NFVWEUmDF).

You can also use GitHub's or GitLab's own **Report** option on any comment or user. We'll keep reports confidential, review every one, and reply within 3 working days. We won't tolerate retaliation against anyone who reports in good faith.

## Enforcement

We'll act in proportion to what happened, taking into account how serious it was and whether it's happened before. We may:

1. **Correct:** a private message explaining what was wrong, sometimes with a request to edit or remove the content.
2. **Warn:** a formal warning, with consequences for repeat behaviour.
3. **Temporarily block:** a time-limited block from interacting with our repositories.
4. **Permanently block:** a permanent block from our repositories, and usually from our Discord and other community spaces too.

Serious breaches, such as threats, doxxing or sexual content involving minors, lead straight to a permanent block and may be reported to the platform or the police. Where behaviour also breaks one of our licences, for example by sharing leaked copies, we may end the licences involved.

## Appeals

If you disagree with a decision, email pigeon@pigeonstudios.co.uk within 14 days, explaining why. We'll look at it again with fresh eyes, taking into account anything new you tell us, and reply with the outcome within 14 days.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in our repositories and wherever someone is representing Pigeon Studios. It also applies to behaviour elsewhere, such as on other Discord servers or social media, when it's directed at people because of their involvement with our projects.
